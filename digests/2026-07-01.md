## 1. 🔔 Cranker-enabling signal (low-confidence — refinement, not new surface)

No new `cfg_*_chunks` knob (1a), no new `*Phase` enum (1b), no funding
apply/settle symbol (1c), no new `crank_/advance_/settle_/apply_` **`pub fn`**
(1d), no A-series liveness roster (1e). What did move is **1f (proof of an
existing crank progress path)** on the resolved-close leg:

- **`228d9b3` "Allow resolved close to wind down unattributed bad debt"**
  (PR [#85](https://github.com/aeyakovenko/percolator/pull/85), `src/v16.rs`
  +53/-29). Adds private helper `clear_resolved_close_leg` and reworks the
  resolved-close accounting so a `CloseResolved` crank step can drain
  *unattributed* bad debt inline instead of stalling into the recovery-fallback
  envelope. **Mechanism for a keeper:** a stuck resolved-close account that
  previously required `DeclareRecovery` before progressing can now be wound down
  directly on the `CloseResolved` plan. All touched fns are private impl
  methods on `MarketGroupV16ViewMut` — **no new public entry point or knob.**
- **`500ebee` "Prove resolved unattributed bad debt progress"** — new proofs
  `proof_v16_resolved_unattributed_bad_debt_clears_without_recovery` and
  `proof_v16_resolved_two_active_legs_are_unattributed_for_bankruptcy`
  (`tests/proofs_v16.rs`). Pins the above liveness claim before it ships —
  same "prove the crank path, then relax it" pattern as the 2026-06-26
  no-observation work.
- **`c8aab33` "Treat finalized close ledgers as inert"**
  (PR [#86](https://github.com/aeyakovenko/percolator/pull/86)). Finalized
  close ledgers no longer participate in liquidation/close dispatch — the
  terminal state of the close lifecycle is now a hard no-op. Relevant to a
  keeper's stop condition: a finalized ledger will report no work rather than
  re-entering the crank loop.

**Sizing:** this is the `CloseResolved` crank path getting *more capable*
(fewer stall-into-recovery cases) and *better bounded* (finalized = inert), not
a new crankable operation. Logged to `signals.md §1f`. Err-toward-flagging per
task rules; confidence deliberately marked low.
