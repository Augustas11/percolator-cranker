This is the first *new* crank-shaped `pub fn` the watch has recorded (previous
crank surface was contraction — the #92 `close_q` removal). It bundles
`build_actionable_summary` + `auto_crank_selected_assets` + `select_auto_crank_plan`
into one call returning the exact `AutoCrankPlanV16` the engine will accept, so a
wrapper "authenticate[s] the observation for the exact engine-selected
continuation without duplicating lifecycle logic" (PR body). **Why this matters
for the reference-cranker thesis:** `prep.md` already flags that a cranker MUST
call the engine's `auto_crank_plan_requires_caller_observation` predicate and NOT
hand-roll the plan match; `build_auto_crank_plan` is the engine centralizing the
*whole* selection step behind one entry point. If it lands, the reference cranker
gets materially simpler and less likely to drift from engine lifecycle rules —
directly favorable to the "open-source reference cranker others fork" half of the
positioning. **Status: DRAFT, not on `master`.** Same PR also fixes an auto-crank
livelock (selects the first `Active`/`DrainOnly` leg, skips `Recovery` legs) —
supersedes the older #98.

**② Funding K/F settlement made crank-cadence-invariant + layout discriminator
17→18 (PR [#129](https://github.com/aeyakovenko/percolator/pull/129),
signal-type 1c).** Branch `codex/fix-funding-crank-cadence-20260716`. The bug:
a permissionless caller could refresh a victim after every sub-atom K/F move, so
ten cranks over an interval charged 10 atoms where one crank charged 1, and
frequent cranks could *erase* otherwise-nonzero funding (accrual divided before
applying index precision). The fix carries persistent Euclidean **K and F
remainders per leg**, preserves them across resize/leg transforms, applies
funding index precision *before* settlement rounding, and **bumps
`V16_LAYOUT_DISCRIMINATOR` 17 → 18** (`src/v16.rs`). This is the funding-crank
surface `prep.md` and the calibration notes told me to watch: it moves funding
from "counters that a naive crank cadence can distort" toward "settlement
invariant to how often the crank runs" — a correctness property a paid cranker
operator would need. No new `pub fn`, no new `cfg_*_chunks`, no new phase enum —
it hardens the existing accrual path. Wrapper LiteSVM repro is
[percolator-prog PR #247](https://github.com/aeyakovenko/percolator-prog/pull/247).
**Status: DRAFT.** The discriminator bump is an ABI-version signal — see §9.

**③ Auto-crank landing-order / pinning correctness (PRs
[#133](https://github.com/aeyakovenko/percolator/pull/133),
[#128](https://github.com/aeyakovenko/percolator/pull/128)).**
- **#133** "Preserve accrual slots without observations": a stale permissionless
  auto-crank landing before the legitimate Hybrid observation could consume the
  positive-time accrual slot and suppress the honest observation, pinning Hybrid
  oracle progress. Fix preserves account-action progress on the committed price
  and only authorizes post-action market accrual when the selected observation
  was actually supplied.
- **#128** "Prevent zero-delta oracle crank pinning": when the per-slot % cap
  rounds below one price atom, a caller could crank every slot, advance
  `slot_last`, and erase elapsed time forever — freezing price-dependent PnL,
  liquidation, and funding. Fix returns `NonProgress` *before* mutation so SVM
  rollback preserves `slot_last`.

Both are anti-grief hardening of the permissionless crank an operator would run.
**Status: DRAFT.**

**④ Funding-lifecycle asset reuse (PR
[#124](https://github.com/aeyakovenko/percolator/pull/124), signal-type 1c-adj).**
A normally-funded asset became permanently unreusable after its lifecycle

public DoS on finite market-slot capacity. Fix treats current/epoch-start F as
inert settlement anchors once all claimant/value fields are empty. Funding-state
lifecycle, DRAFT.
