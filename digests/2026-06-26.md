# Percolator Watch — 2026-06-26

(`3d250d2`, `f5d291e`, `8338726`) that lets the permissionless auto-crank

## 1. 🔔 Cranker-enabling signal

**Auto-crank now progresses without a caller observation (Liquidate / B-settle).**
Three merged commits, all Anatoly via codex tooling (PRs
[#82](https://github.com/aeyakovenko/percolator/pull/82),
[#83](https://github.com/aeyakovenko/percolator/pull/83)):

- **`3d250d2` "Allow auto-crank liquidation without fresh observations"**
  (`src/v16.rs` +17). Adds an `obs_or_current_asset` fallback closure: when
  `work.observations` lacks an entry for the asset, the dispatcher synthesizes
  an `AutoCrankObservationV16` from committed `asset_state` (effective_price,
  `funding_rate_e9: 0`). The `SettleBChunk` and `Liquidate` arms switch from
  `obs_for(asset_index)?` (which errored `NonProgress` on a missing obs) to
  `obs_or_current_asset(self, asset_index)?`. **Mechanism:** a keeper can now
  liquidate / settle-B-chunk a stuck account using on-chain committed price
  rather than supplying a signed fresh observation.
  Diff lines: `src/v16.rs:11328` (closure), `:11385` & `:11395` (call-site swaps).

- **`f5d291e` "Close the auto-crank no-DoS dispatch seam"** (`src/v16.rs` +,
  `tests/proofs_v16.rs` +28). Adds the free **`pub fn
  auto_crank_plan_requires_caller_observation(plan: &AutoCrankPlanV16) -> bool`**
  (`src/v16.rs:187`), **exported in `src/lib.rs:54`** — i.e. wrapper-usable.
  Its exhaustive match makes `RefreshAccount{asset_index: None}` the **unique**
  plan that hard-requires a caller observation; every other variant
  (`SettleBChunk`, `Liquidate`, `DeclareRecovery`, `CloseResolved`, `NoAction`,
  and `RefreshAccount{Some}`) is dispatchable from committed state. New proof
  `proof_v16_auto_crank_refresh_is_unique_observation_requiring_plan` pins the
  six-variant truth table; a new plan variant breaks the match.

- **`8338726` "Allow active auto-crank refresh without observation"**
  (`src/v16.rs` +40/-, `tests/proofs_v16.rs`, `tests/v16_spec_tests.rs`).
  Extends the no-observation path to active `RefreshAccount{Some(asset_index)}`
  (refresh of a *specific* already-known asset) — only the open-ended
  `RefreshAccount{None}` (must accrue a brand-new price) still stalls
  `NonProgress` without an observation.

**Signal-type mapping:** 1d (new public crank-path surface: the exported
predicate fn) + 1f (new crank-path proofs/conformance). No new `cfg_*_chunks`
knob (1a), no new `*Phase` enum (1b), no new funding symbol (1c), no new
`crank_`/`settle_`/`apply_`-prefixed `pub fn` (1d-prefix sense). `AutoCrankPlanV16`
variant **set unchanged** (6 variants); `RefreshAccount` now carries
`{ asset_index: Option<usize> }`.

**Why it matters for the thesis:** the reference cranker no longer needs an
authenticated-observation pipeline to *liquidate* or *settle B-chunks* — only
to *refresh a new price*. That removes the hardest dependency (oracle signing /
hint routing) from the most common crank actions, simplifying both the
publishable reference and operator uptime. `signals.md` §1d/§1f updated.
